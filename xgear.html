Product Creatives

Brand Commercial

FORMAT: 1080P • 24 FPS COLOR: REC.709 SOFTWARE: PR • AE ROLE: CONCEPT • CINEMATOGRAPHY • EDIT • MOTION
Brief & Objective

Brand Identity Showcase: Designed as a punchy brand commercial to highlight the core values, modern tech ecosystem, and premium shopping experience that The New Xgear delivers to gamers and creators.

Visual & Cinematography Approach

Dynamic Handheld & Gimbal Work: Captured using digital cinema cameras paired with a 3-axis gimbal to achieve fluid, cinematic camera moves. The setup balances real-world retail store ambience with practical product lighting to emphasize sleek hardware contours and premium textures.

Pacing & Editorial Flow

Rhythm-Driven Editing: Crafted as a fast-paced 60-second commercial. Rather than intricate sound layering, the edit relies on a driving, continuous music track, cutting precisely on the musical beats to keep viewer retention high and maintain high-energy momentum throughout.
